# Final Analytical Report

## Introduction
Artificial Intelligence (AI) has become an increasingly important component of modern education systems. From assisting students in learning tasks to supporting teachers and institutions in educational processes, AI tools are now widely integrated into academic environments. As AI adoption continues to grow, understanding the accessibility, usage patterns, and structural factors influencing adoption becomes increasingly relevant.

This analysis explores global AI usage in education using data collected from 10 countries between January 2015 and April 2026. The study focuses on usage trends, adoption patterns, infrastructural influence, and educational development indicators associated with AI adoption.

---

## Business / Analytical Questions
- How does AI tool preference evolve over time?
- How does AI tool preference vary across countries?
- How does AI usage evolve over time across different user groups?
- How do countries compare in terms of internet penetration and education index?
- How does internet penetration relate to AI usage patterns?
- How does education index relate to AI adoption in educational systems?

---

## Methodology
### Data Cleaning
The dataset was cleaned by handling missing values, checking duplicate entries, validating categorical inconsistencies, and performing necessary data transformations for analysis.

### Aggregation and Summary Analysis
Country-level and yearly aggregations were created to summarize AI usage trends and support comparative analysis across different educational and infrastructural metrics.

### Visualization
Visualizations were used to identify temporal trends, structural relationships, and comparative patterns across countries and user groups.

### Comparative Analysis
Comparative analysis was performed to evaluate differences in AI adoption across educational groups, infrastructural conditions, and country-level development indicators.

---

## Key Findings
### 1. AI Usage Trends Over Time
Urban and rural AI usage both increase over time, though urban usage consistently remains higher. Similarly, student AI usage, teacher AI usage, and school-level AI adoption all exhibit upward trends across years. Gender gap in AI usage remains relatively stable with limited temporal variation.

### 2. Urban vs Rural Usage Patterns
Urban AI usage consistently exceeds rural AI usage across all observed years. The urban–rural usage gap widens during the initial years before stabilizing, suggesting gradual improvement in rural accessibility to AI tools over time.

### 3. Student vs Teacher AI Usage
Student AI usage consistently exceeds teacher AI usage, indicating faster adoption among students. Both student and teacher usage remain higher than school-level institutional adoption, though the alignment between student usage and institutional adoption appears stronger and more consistent.

### 4. Gender Gap in AI Usage
Gender gap in AI usage remains within a relatively narrow range across years with no clear temporal trend. This suggests that gender-based differences in AI usage remain comparatively stable over time.

### 5. Internet Penetration and AI Usage
Countries with higher internet penetration generally exhibit higher AI usage levels and smaller urban–rural usage gaps. However, internet penetration shows limited visible association with gender-based usage differences and average daily AI usage duration.

### 6. Education Index and AI Adoption
Countries with higher education index values generally exhibit stronger institutional AI adoption and higher AI usage among students and teachers. Lower education index countries show comparatively higher variability in usage metrics, indicating less consistent adoption patterns.

---

## Overall Conclusions
- Infrastructural development appears strongly associated with increased accessibility and adoption of AI technologies in education.
- Institutional AI adoption shows strong alignment with student usage trends.
- Structural differences across user groups are more pronounced than overall cross-country differences.
- Internet penetration and educational development both demonstrate meaningful association with AI adoption patterns.

---

## Limitations
- The dataset includes only 10 countries and may not fully represent global AI adoption patterns.
- The analysis is observational and does not establish causal relationships.
- Aggregated metrics summarize trends at a higher level and may not capture detailed local variations or demographic-level patterns.
- Some country-level indicators remain structurally fixed within the dataset, limiting deeper temporal comparison.
- Data for 2026 spans over four months making it non-suitable for analysis purposes
